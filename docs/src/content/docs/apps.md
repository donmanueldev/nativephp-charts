## Despertá

[Despertá](https://desperta.momotombo.dev/) is an Android alarm app built with NativePHP Mobile that uses NativePHP Charts.

Users set a repeating alarm, answer three questions to dismiss it, and track their habit history. Alarms and habits are stored locally on the device; no account is required.

- [Visit the Despertá website](https://desperta.momotombo.dev/)
- [View Despertá on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=dev.momotombo.desperta)

## Add charts to your app

Follow the [installation guide](/nativephp-charts/getting-started/installation/) to register the plugin and create a native screen, or [choose a chart type](/nativephp-charts/getting-started/overview/#choose-a-chart) for your data.
